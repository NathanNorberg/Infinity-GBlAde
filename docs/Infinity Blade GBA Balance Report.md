# Infinity Blade GBA: Balance Report

Generated by `tools/balance/report` from the game's data headers (tuning.h, item_roster.h, castle.h). Regenerate after changing them:

```bash
tools/balance/build.sh && tools/balance/report > "docs/Infinity Blade GBA Balance Report.md"
```

**Model:** the hero wins every duel and takes every treasure, alternating the garden and barracks paths, with no XP bonuses and no chest items, and beats the God King at the end of every run, so each run is one cycle further on (every enemy gains 20% HP, 10% damage and 30% XP and gold per cycle; the God King 25% HP). Stat points alternate Health and Attack; after each run the hero buys the best affordable weapon, armor, helmet and shield. It's optimistic (real runs end in deaths, which don't advance the cycle), so read it for outliers and pacing, not exact numbers.

**Columns:** *Slashes* is hits to kill the enemy at the hero's slash damage (combos, supers and spells shorten it). *Hits to die* is the hero's max HP over the enemy's average hit after armor, with unblockables weighted in; in a run, HP carries between duels, healing 10% per win.
